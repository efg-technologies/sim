sim tutor: short lessons, about 15 minutes
==========================================
This is a scratch copy. Nothing you do here is written to a disk, so
experiment freely. Press u to undo; type :q! and Enter to leave.

Lines that start with [ ] are exercises. Do what they ask: the box
turns from ✗ to ✓ as soon as the text is right (and back if you undo).

Scroll with j and k, or Ctrl-d and Ctrl-u for half a page. To jump to
a lesson, search for its title line: /^## 3 and Enter (^ is the start
of a line, so only the title matches).

## 1 Moving around

h moves left, j down, k up, l right. The arrow keys work too.
w goes to the start of the next word, b back to the start of a word.
0 goes to the start of the line, $ to its end.
gg goes to the first line of the file, G to the last.
A number repeats a move: 3j goes down three lines.

[ ] 1.1 Move to the letter x (w or l) and press the x key to delete.
a b c d x e f g
@ a b c d  e f g

[ ] 1.2 Go down 3 lines (3j), press $, delete the last character (x).
one
two
three!
@ one
@ two
@ three

## 2 Typing text

i starts typing before the cursor, a after it. I types at the start
of the line, A at its end. o opens a new line below, O above.
Esc ends typing. When unsure what mode you are in, press Esc: the
bar at the bottom of the screen names the mode.

[ ] 2.1 On the 5, press i, type the missing 4 and a space, then Esc.
1 2 3 5 6
@ 1 2 3 4 5 6

[ ] 2.2 Add " 3" at the end of the line with A, then Esc.
1 2
@ 1 2 3

[ ] 2.3 Open a line below the 1 with o, type 2, then Esc.
1
3
@ 1
@ 2
@ 3

## 3 Deleting and changing

x deletes the character under the cursor, dw a word, dd a line.
cw changes a word: it deletes it and starts typing. r replaces one
character. d and c take any move: d$ deletes to the end of the line,
c2w changes two words.

[ ] 3.1 Delete the second "two" with dw.
one two two three
@ one two three

[ ] 3.2 Delete the line of x's with dd.
keep
xxxxx
keep
@ keep
@ keep

[ ] 3.3 Change the 9 to a 3 with cw (or r).
1 2 9 4
@ 1 2 3 4

## 4 Undo

u undoes the last change, Ctrl-r redoes what you undid. Both take a
count: 3u undoes three changes.

[ ] 4.1 Delete two words, then bring one back (lessons 3 and 4).
1 2 3
@ 2 3

## 5 Copy and paste

yy copies a line, p pastes it below the cursor, P above it. yw copies
a word. What dd and dw delete is copied too: that is how text moves.

[ ] 5.1 Copy the line with yy and paste it below with p.
a b c
@ a b c
@ a b c

[ ] 5.2 Move the 1 below the 3 (lessons 1, 3 and 5).
1
2
3
@ 2
@ 3
@ 1

## 6 Searching

/word and Enter find the next "word". n repeats the search, N goes
the other way, * searches for the word under the cursor. :s/a/b/
replaces on this line, :%s/a/b/g in the whole file. :noh clears the
lit matches.

[ ] 6.1 Search /zq7 (n: next match), then delete the word found.
a b c d
e f g h
i j zq7 k
l m n o
@ a b c d
@ e f g h
@ i j k
@ l m n o

[ ] 6.2 Replace every a with b on the line below: :s/a/b/g and Enter.
a a a
@ b b b

## 7 Saving and quitting

:w saves, :q quits, :wq does both, :q! quits and throws changes away.
sim never loses text without your say: before :w! or :e! throws a
version away (yours or the disk's), it copies that version to its own
folder. If another program changes your file while you edit, sim
merges the change or shows the conflict; it never overwrites silently.
This tutor is a scratch copy: :w says no and writes nothing.

[ ] 7.1 Put the lines in order (lessons 3 and 5).
c 3
a 1
b 2
@ a 1
@ b 2
@ c 3

## 8 What sim shows that other editors hide

A character that is not shown as itself (a control, a zero-width
space, a right-to-left override) is drawn as a token such as <U+202E>
or ^[ in a colored block, so it cannot hide in a file.

[ ] 8.1 Delete the hidden character shown as a token.
safe\u{200B}word
@ safeword

In a Markdown file, a link whose text names one address while its
target is another is marked, so a link cannot say one place and go to
another: [https://good.example](https://evil.example)
%editor

## 9 Finding files and looks

Ctrl-p opens the file finder: type part of a name, Enter opens it.
Ctrl-f searches inside the files of the project. Esc closes either.
Ctrl-n shows or hides the tree. zl switches the look (calm, flat,
depth); zi turns the icons on or off (they need a Nerd Font).
<Space>bk tells where sim put the last version it kept. Press Space
and wait: a menu names every key that can follow.

[ ] 9.1 Open the file finder with Ctrl-p, then close it with Esc.
@! finder
[ ] 9.2 Search the files with Ctrl-f, then close it with Esc.
@! grep
[ ] 9.3 Show or hide the tree with Ctrl-n.
@! tree
[ ] 9.4 Switch the look with zl.
@! look
[ ] 9.5 Ask for the last kept version with <Space>bk.
@! kept
%end

That is all. :q! leaves the tutor; :help lists every key.
